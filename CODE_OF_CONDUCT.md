# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participation is welcome regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the boundaries of repository owners.
- Keep review feedback focused on the work rather than the person.
- Correct mistakes openly and help others learn safely.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates these expectations. Repeated or serious violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue when a report
contains personal, security-sensitive, or otherwise private information.
